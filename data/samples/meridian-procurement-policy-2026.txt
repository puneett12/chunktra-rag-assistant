MERIDIAN INDUSTRIES INC.
PROCUREMENT POLICY 2026

Owner: Office of the Chief Financial Officer
Effective: 1 January 2026
Applies to: all employees who commit company funds or sign supplier agreements


1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy governs how Meridian Industries selects, contracts with and manages
third-party suppliers. It applies to all purchases of goods and services regardless of
value, and to all renewals and amendments of existing supplier agreements.

1.2 Deviations from this policy require written approval from the Chief Financial Officer
in advance of commitment. Retrospective approval is not permitted.


2. AUTHORITY TO COMMIT

2.1 Spend authority is as follows:

    Up to $25,000 .................. Department Manager
    $25,001 to $250,000 ............ Director of Procurement
    $250,001 to $1,000,000 ......... Chief Financial Officer
    Above $1,000,000 ............... Board Audit Committee

2.2 A multi-year commitment is assessed at its total contract value, not its annual
value, for the purposes of Section 2.1.

2.3 Splitting a purchase into smaller transactions to remain below an approval threshold
is a disciplinary matter.


3. COMPETITIVE SOURCING

3.1 Purchases above $100,000 require a minimum of three written quotations, or a
documented sole-source justification approved by the Director of Procurement.

3.2 Sole-source justifications are valid for twenty-four (24) months and must be renewed
before any subsequent award to the same supplier.


4. CONTRACT TERMS

4.1 Term length. New supplier agreements shall not exceed an initial term of three (3)
years without CFO approval.

4.2 Price escalation. Annual price escalation clauses shall not exceed the lesser of CPI
or three percent (3%) per year. Any escalation clause exceeding this ceiling requires
written CFO approval and must be recorded as a policy exception in the contract register.

4.3 Notice periods. Agreements containing automatic renewal provisions must provide the
Company with a termination notice window of no less than sixty (60) days and no more than
one hundred eighty (180) days before the renewal date.

4.4 Liability. Supplier liability caps below twelve (12) months of fees require Legal
review. Unlimited liability carve-outs for confidentiality, data protection and
indemnification are mandatory.

4.5 Governing law. Agreements shall be governed by the law of a United States
jurisdiction unless Legal approves otherwise in writing.


5. RENEWAL MANAGEMENT

5.1 The contract owner is responsible for reviewing each agreement no later than one
hundred twenty (120) days before its renewal date.

5.2 The renewal review must assess: supplier performance against service levels, current
market pricing, continued business need, and whether the agreement's terms remain
compliant with this policy.

5.3 Where the Company intends not to renew, written notice must be issued no later than
five (5) business days before the contractual notice deadline, to allow for delivery and
receipt.

5.4 An agreement that renews automatically because notice was missed is a reportable
control failure and must be logged with the Office of the CFO within ten (10) business
days.


6. SUPPLIER RISK

6.1 Suppliers processing Company personal data must complete the Data Protection
Assessment before contract signature and annually thereafter.

6.2 Suppliers with annual spend above $500,000 are subject to an annual financial
viability review.


7. CONTRACT REGISTER

7.1 All executed agreements, amendments and policy exceptions must be filed in the
contract register within five (5) business days of signature.

7.2 The contract register is the system of record for renewal dates, notice deadlines and
approved exceptions.
