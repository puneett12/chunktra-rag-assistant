MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 4 March 2024
(the "Effective Date") by and between Meridian Industries Inc., a Delaware corporation
with offices at 400 Harbor Street, Wilmington, Delaware ("Customer"), and ABC Company
LLC, a New York limited liability company with offices at 118 Lexington Avenue,
New York, New York ("Supplier").

Customer contact for notices: R. Mehta, Director of Procurement.
Supplier contact for notices: contracts@abc-company.com.


1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the professional, managed and support services described in each
Statement of Work executed by the parties under this Agreement.

1.2 "Statement of Work" or "SOW" means a document describing specific Services,
deliverables, timelines and fees, executed by authorised representatives of both parties
and incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

1.3 "Deliverables" means any reports, software, documentation or other work product
that Supplier is required to deliver to Customer under a Statement of Work.

1.4 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to
the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.


2. SERVICES

2.1 Supplier shall perform the Services in accordance with each applicable Statement of
Work and in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

2.2 No Statement of Work shall be binding until executed by both parties. In the event
of a conflict between this Agreement and any Statement of Work, the terms of this
Agreement shall control unless the Statement of Work expressly states otherwise and is
signed by an officer of each party.

2.3 Supplier shall not subcontract performance of any material portion of the Services
without Customer's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.


3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay the fees set out in each Statement of Work. Unless otherwise
specified, fees are quoted in United States dollars and exclude applicable taxes.

3.2 Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Undisputed invoices are payable
within forty-five (45) days of receipt.

3.3 Annual fees shall increase upon each renewal term by the lesser of the increase in
the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) over the preceding twelve
months or three percent (3%).

3.4 Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith, provided it
notifies Supplier of the dispute in writing within thirty (30) days of the invoice date
and pays all undisputed amounts when due.


4. TRAVEL AND EXPENSES

4.1 Customer shall reimburse Supplier for reasonable, pre-approved travel and
out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing the Services, provided such expenses are
submitted with supporting receipts within sixty (60) days of being incurred.


5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using at least the
degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less
than a reasonable degree of care.

5.2 The obligations in this Section 5 survive termination of this Agreement for a period
of five (5) years, except with respect to trade secrets, for which the obligations
survive for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.


6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Customer retains all right, title and interest in Customer materials provided to
Supplier. Supplier retains all right, title and interest in its pre-existing tools,
methodologies and know-how.

6.2 Upon full payment, Supplier assigns to Customer all right, title and interest in the
Deliverables created specifically for Customer under a Statement of Work, excluding any
Supplier pre-existing materials incorporated therein, for which Supplier grants Customer
a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to use such materials as part of the
Deliverables.


7. WARRANTIES

7.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner and that the Deliverables will materially conform to the
specifications in the applicable Statement of Work for ninety (90) days following
delivery.

7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, SUPPLIER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER
WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND
FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.


8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or
punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or relating to this
Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

8.2 Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement
shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer under the applicable
Statement of Work during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving
rise to the claim.

8.3 The limitations in this Section 8 do not apply to a party's indemnification
obligations under Section 9, to breaches of Section 5 (Confidentiality), or to damages
arising from a party's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.


9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer against any third-party
claim alleging that the Deliverables infringe a United States patent, copyright or trade
secret, and shall pay any damages finally awarded or amounts agreed in settlement.

9.2 Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier against any third-party
claim arising from Customer materials supplied to Supplier for use in the Services.


10. DATA PROTECTION

10.1 Supplier shall process Customer personal data only on documented instructions from
Customer and shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect
such data.

10.2 Supplier shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within
seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer
personal data.


11. INSURANCE

11.1 Supplier shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability insurance
of not less than two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence and professional
liability insurance of not less than five million dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate.


12. TERM AND TERMINATION

12.1 Initial Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an
initial term of three (3) years, expiring on 14 February 2027, unless terminated earlier
in accordance with this Section 12.

12.2 Statements of Work. Each Statement of Work continues for the period stated therein.
Expiration or termination of this Agreement terminates all outstanding Statements of
Work unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

12.3 Renewal and Notice. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive
one-year terms unless either party gives ninety (90) days written notice prior to the end
of the then-current term. Notice given under this Section 12.3 must be delivered in
accordance with Section 14 and is effective upon receipt.

12.4 Termination for Convenience. After the Initial Term, either party may terminate this
Agreement for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days written notice, provided
that Customer shall remain liable for fees for Services performed through the effective
date of termination.

12.5 Termination for Breach. In the event of a material breach, the non-breaching party
may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days written notice, provided the breaching
party has failed to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice
describing the breach in reasonable detail.

12.6 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Supplier shall promptly return or destroy
Customer Confidential Information, deliver all completed Deliverables for which Customer
has paid, and provide reasonable transition assistance for up to sixty (60) days at
Supplier's then-current time and materials rates.


13. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to
its conflict of laws principles.

13.2 The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through negotiation
between senior executives for thirty (30) days before commencing litigation. Any suit
shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington,
Delaware, and each party consents to the jurisdiction of such courts.


14. NOTICES

14.1 All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by
certified mail return receipt requested, by nationally recognised overnight courier, or
by email with written confirmation of receipt, to the contacts identified above.

14.2 Notices are deemed given upon receipt if delivered by hand or courier, three (3)
business days after mailing if sent by certified mail, or upon confirmation of receipt if
sent by email.


15. GENERAL

15.1 Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior
written consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of
substantially all assets.

15.2 Force Majeure. Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by
events beyond its reasonable control, provided it gives prompt notice and uses
commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

15.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work and
amendments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior
understandings relating to its subject matter.

15.4 Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by
authorised representatives of both parties.
