AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 2 (the "Amendment") is made as of 11 January 2026 between Meridian
Industries Inc. ("Customer") and ABC Company LLC ("Supplier"), and amends the Master
Services Agreement between the parties dated 4 March 2024 (the "Agreement").

Capitalised terms not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given in the Agreement.


1. AMENDMENTS

1.1 Scope Expansion. Schedule A to Statement of Work 7 is amended to include managed
monitoring of Customer's North American data centres, effective 1 February 2026.

1.2 Fee Escalation. Section 3.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced
with the following:

    "3.3 Annual fees shall increase upon each renewal term by the lesser of the increase
    in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) over the preceding twelve
    months or four percent (4%)."

1.3 Service Levels. A new Section 2.4 is added to the Agreement:

    "2.4 Supplier shall maintain monthly availability of not less than 99.5% for the
    monitored services. For each full percentage point below that threshold, Customer
    shall receive a service credit equal to five percent (5%) of the monthly fee for the
    affected services, capped at twenty-five percent (25%) of that monthly fee. Service
    credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures."


2. NO OTHER CHANGES

Except as expressly amended above, all terms and conditions of the Agreement remain in
full force and effect. For the avoidance of doubt, this Amendment does not alter the
Initial Term, the renewal mechanics or the notice period set out in Section 12 of the
Agreement.


3. COUNTERPARTS

This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original and
all of which together constitute one instrument.
